What this tells us

Removing memories lets us investigate whether past experience changes later behaviour. A zero difference is a valid result. If no procedural memories were acquired, removing them cannot test their usefulness.

A convincing learning result also needs a defined follow-up challenge, independently verified success, matched controls and repeated real-model trials. Those evaluations are not included in this first comparison.
